# SegmentScraper v1.12.7 — Available episodes and timestamp review

This update improves episode counting, IMDb lookup and timestamp review in the userscript, with applicable review and lookup improvements in Desktop Alpha 0.1.

## Features

- Add manual Intro/Recap/Outro marking from the userscript player panel, with explicit title/episode confirmation, start/end marks and short boundary previews. The panel starts collapsed on every provider.
- Filter timestamp comparison, JSON export and upload approval by segment type, including desktop comparison. Changing the filter clears approvals.
- In the userscript, collect Prime season episode lists and the complete Videoland programme catalogue without requiring playback of every episode just to count them. Videoland follows section and episode pagination, batches up to eight episode pages and falls back to individual pages when batching is unsupported.

## Bug fixes

- When episode totals differ, allow TVDB episodes dated in the future and undated placeholders absent from matching provider numbering to be excluded. Episodes already available from the provider still count even if TVDB has a later or unknown date. Malformed dates and conflicting metadata do not excuse a mismatch.
- Verify missing dates against individual TVDB episode records where possible, refresh failed mappings on retry, and show the actual mismatch and extra episode numbers in timestamp review.
- Wait for the Videoland catalogue before validating export/upload. Detect repeated pagination contents, stop failed/partial catalogue checks and identify the affected programme, season and cursor in errors.
- Retry TV titles ending in “De Serie” or “The Series” without that suffix only when the original search finds no result of the requested media type. Require an exact shortened title and, for automatic lookup, the supplied year. This resolves Videoland's “Gooische Vrouwen De Serie” to the TV series rather than the films.
- Preserve revised timestamp observations while deduplicating exact repeats. Competing ranges require a reviewed selection, and changed captures invalidate stale approvals.
- Preserve valid zero-second starts; reject missing, invalid, reversed and out-of-duration boundaries. Pause manual marking at the actual player clock and handle interrupted previews safely.
