# SegmentScraper v1.12.3

## Userscript

- Correct Netflix movie credits starts by six seconds while preserving the Netflix runtime end.
- Capture Netflix movie outros and keep online movie protection for known mid-/post-credits scenes.
- Recognize Prime Video after-, post-, mid- and during-credits markers.
- Clear captured data after a confirmed JSON download or a fully successful IntroDB submission; failed submissions remain available for retry.

## Desktop Alpha 0.1

The desktop app is published as an experimental alpha for testing. It includes the reviewed movie-ending analysis, short boundary previews, local workspaces, duplicate checks and explicit upload review. Review every suggested boundary against the actual video before uploading and keep backups of important reports.
